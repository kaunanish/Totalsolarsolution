Government Scheme

PM Surya Ghar: Muft Bijli Yojana

TSS helps you apply for and install solar under the Government of India's flagship rooftop solar scheme — so you save more and pay less, right from day one.

  • Up to ₹1,38,000 central subsidy — ₹30,000/kW for the first 2kW and ₹18,000 for the 3rd kW.
  • Up to 300 units of free electricity every month for eligible households.
  • Subsidy is credited directly to your bank account within 30 days of commissioning.
  • Available for residential connections with sanctioned load up to 10 kW.
  • Applied through the official national portal, coordinated by MNRE via your local DISCOM.
